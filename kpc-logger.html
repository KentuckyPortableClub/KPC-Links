Download & files

POTA wants one file per park (CALL@PARK-DATE.adi): upload at pota.app → My Logs. WWFF / KFF gets one ADIF file (CALL@KFF-0000 DATE.adi) for your WWFF log manager. SOTA gets the sotadata CSV: upload at sotadata.org.uk.

Install it · use it with no signal

On a computer (Chrome or Edge): tap 📲 INSTALL THE LOGGER above, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar. It opens in its own window like a program.

On an iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share (on newer iPhones •••, then Share) → Add to Home Screen → Add.

On Android (Chrome): tap 📲 INSTALL THE LOGGER above, or ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app.

No signal at the park? Tap 💾 SAVE FOR NO SIGNAL first, with your park set (it also saves the park list for PARKS NEAR ME and the street map around your park). With no signal you can still log, see your rate and notes, find nearby parks by GPS and download your POTA files. Spots, call look-ups, self-spotting and the map pictures need signal; calls you've looked up before are remembered.

Call sign details come from public FCC data (callook.info, HamDB). Your log stays on this device (two copies) until you download it. KPC is an independent ham radio club, not part of Parks on the Air®.
