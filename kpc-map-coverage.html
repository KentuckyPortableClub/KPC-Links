"Missing" = no official layer found yet, or a near-miss that needs a hand check. Pin-only parks (a historic site, trailhead or similar single-spot park) and parks with an official trail/river line are not missing — they have the right kind of record for what they are. "% complete" counts every column a park could have filled in (map, rules, hours, fees, access point, who runs it, size check, campground), not just the outline. These counts are updated every night.
