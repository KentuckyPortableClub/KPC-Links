Tuner, laptop, phone charging, lights… a phone charging is about 0.5–1 A at 12 V.

How it adds up
Field tips
  • Plan for about 70% of the estimate — cold weather, an older battery and a high SWR all shorten runtime.
  • Many radios shut down or distort as voltage sags. Lithium (LiFePO4) holds its voltage much better than lead-acid.
  • Dropping from 100 W to 50 W roughly halves transmit current. On SSB, the difference is barely noticeable to the station on the other end.
  • Charge a battery fully the night before, and bring a backup for long activations.
Estimates only. Average current is receive current while listening, plus transmit current while transmitting — reduced for SSB and CW because voice and keying don’t draw full power the whole time — plus any other gear. Check your battery’s and radio’s own specifications.
