    Where this comes from

    This planner covers the whole US. Park types, official websites and how you can activate come from POTA’s park list, refreshed every week; campgrounds and picnic shelters come from Recreation.gov (federal land only, so check state parks on their own site); SOTA summits come from SOTA and WWFF references from the WWFF directory. Boundaries on the map are the official USGS PAD-US outlines; when a park has none, its outline is from OpenStreetMap and is marked NOT OFFICIAL. For Kentucky, the full KPC Trip Planner adds hand-checked access points, 2-fers and more. Place search by OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Routes open in Google Maps.
    Use our tools, then check for yourself. KPC builds these maps, guides and tools to help you plan, but we’re volunteers working from public data, and we make mistakes. Boundaries change, maps don’t always line up, and some land inside a park line is private. Before you activate, check the official agency map, confirm the land is public and open, and follow the rules of each program you’re activating. You’re responsible for making sure your activation is valid. Spot something wrong? Let us know so we can fix it.
