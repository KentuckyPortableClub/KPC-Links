BE PART OF KPC.

KPC is just getting started, and we're not quite ready to open membership yet. But you don't have to wait to be part of what we're building. Everyone is welcome to participate with us — come to an event, join us for a campout, get outside, get on the air, and connect with other operators.

Membership Waitlist
