SUMMITS ON THE AIR

Hike to the top of a listed mountain, set up a small station and make contacts, or chase the people who do. Kentucky's summits are in the W4K association.

WHAT IT IS

SOTA in a Minute

Activators carry a station to a listed summit and make contacts from the top. Chasers work them. Every summit has a reference like W4K/EC-001 and a points value set by its height. Summits are picked by how much they stand out from the land around them, not just how high they are.

25 m(80 ft) below the top: the activation zone
1contact logs an activation
4contacts earn the summit's points
0cars: no operating from or next to a vehicle

THE RULES

📜 The Main Rules

Our plain-words summary (KPC wrote it; it is not official). Read the SOTA General Rules and the W4K (Kentucky) Association Reference Manual before you go.

  • Activation zone: you must be within 25 m (about 80 ft) of height below the very top of the summit.
  • 1 contact is enough to log an activation; 4 contacts are needed to earn the summit's points. Making more doesn't add points.
  • No vehicles: you can't operate from or close to a vehicle, and the trip up has to be on foot (or under your own power). Use legitimate access routes and follow the local land rules.
  • Log every contact and upload your log to the SOTA database (sotadata).
  • Chasers log their contacts in the SOTA database too, to earn chaser points.
  • Points for each summit are in the summit list; some associations add a seasonal bonus. Check the W4K manual for Kentucky's.
  • Same ground as a POTA park? A summit inside a POTA park is a different program: you can log both, but that's not a POTA 2-fer. For POTA you must still be inside the park boundary.

Sources: sota.org.uk (General Rules and the W4K Association Reference Manual).

These are short, plain-language summaries to get you started. The program's own rules always win: read the official pages linked here before you go, because rules can change.

KPC is an independent ham radio club. We are not part of Summits on the Air and this is not official SOTA material: the official site is sota.org.uk.

HOW TO START

🚀 Your First Summit or Chase

⛰ Activating

  1. Pick a summit: our Kentucky SOTA guide or the SOTA Summit Map.
  2. Post an alert on SOTAwatch and the Activation Board, and check Field Weather.
  3. Hike up. At the top, Am I in the Park? shows whether you're inside the 25 m activation zone.
  4. Set up away from your vehicle, spot yourself (Spot Me copies the SOTAwatch format) and call CQ SOTA.
  5. Get at least 4 contacts, then upload your log at sotadata.

🎯 Chasing

  1. Watch SOTAwatch for spots and alerts (Happening Now shows SOTA spots too).
  2. Call once with your call sign, give a report, and listen for the summit reference.
  3. Log the contact with the summit reference.
  4. Upload your chaser log at sotadata to get your points.
