KPC Kentucky GIS update — October 8, 2026

Added selectable live government GIS layers:
- Pine Mountain Trail (Open), Kentucky recreational trails layer 1
- Dawkins Line candidate named features, Kentucky Rails to Trails layer 3
- Sheltowee Trace candidate named features, Kentucky Federal Hiking Trails layer 11
- Kentucky State Park trails, state park trailheads, campgrounds, and boundaries
- Existing NPS Trail of Tears and Lewis & Clark layers retained

The GIS request is made by each visitor's browser when they click Load routes in map view, at zoom 8+.
The Kentucky GIS services are not mirrored in this ZIP. This environment could not connect to the GIS servers to download geometry, so live browser behavior and the feature-name matches have NOT been verified.

Every candidate is labeled POSSIBLE 2-fer; a trail line near a POTA coordinate does not establish a second POTA reference, or simultaneous POTA/SOTA/WWFF eligibility. Park boundaries from state GIS are contextual, not necessarily POTA-designated activation boundaries.
