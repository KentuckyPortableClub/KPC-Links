Some POTA results show WWFF reference matches from a January 2025 cross-reference. About this map & offline use

Built for Kentucky. The park boundaries, trails and “Am I inside a park?” check cover Kentucky only for now. Searching and park pins work nationwide, and overlays for other states may be added later.
A planning tool, not the final word. GIS lines can be simplified, out of date or different from what POTA uses, and a boundary on this map doesn’t confirm POTA eligibility, public access or current rules. Before you activate, check the managing agency’s official map, the park’s POTA page, posted rules and closures, and hunting seasons. When in doubt, ask the land manager.

Trails & Nearby Activations

Explore five major Kentucky trails and search for SOTA, POTA and WWFF locations near a trail town or access point. Historic route lines are not necessarily walkable trails or eligible activation areas.

Trail Information Notice: Trail information is currently limited and does not include every hiking, recreational, or historic trail in the United States. Additional trails and mapping data may be added as they become available. Always verify trail routes, access, closures, and activation eligibility with official sources before traveling.
POTA 2-fer notice: A POTA 2-fer requires two distinct qualifying POTA references (US-#### and US-####) at the same eligible activation location. POTA + WWFF/KFF is not a POTA 2-fer. Trail proximity and historical cross-references do not establish a POTA 2-fer; confirm current POTA references and boundaries before activating.
