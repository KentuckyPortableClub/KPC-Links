Trail Information Notice: Trail information is currently limited and does not include every hiking, recreational, or historic trail in the United States. Additional trails and mapping data may be added as they become available. Always verify trail routes, access, closures, and activation eligibility with official sources before traveling.
POTA 2-fer notice: A POTA 2-fer requires two distinct qualifying POTA references (US-#### and US-####) at the same eligible activation location. POTA + WWFF/KFF is not a POTA 2-fer. Trail proximity and historical cross-references do not establish a POTA 2-fer; confirm current POTA references and boundaries before activating.

Cross-program activation opportunities

POTA results display WWFF reference matches from your January 2025 cross-reference. These are documented historical reference cross-references, not verified simultaneous activation eligibility. Summit and park proximity alone cannot confirm SOTA/POTA/WWFF multi-program qualification; verify current official boundaries, access, and each program’s rules before activating.

Official trail map, trailhead, and camping links appear on selected POTA results when an agency resource is identified. Coverage is incomplete; some links open an official map directory rather than a park-specific map. An official map does not verify POTA eligibility.
