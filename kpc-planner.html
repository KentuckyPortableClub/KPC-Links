Use our tools, then check for yourself. KPC builds these maps, guides and tools to help you plan, but we’re volunteers working from public data, and we make mistakes. Boundaries change, maps don’t always line up, and some land inside a park line is private. Before you activate, check the official agency map, confirm the land is public and open, and follow the rules of each program you’re activating. You’re responsible for making sure your activation is valid. Spot something wrong? Let us know so we can fix it.
