⭐ EASIEST: GET MY HUNTED PARKS STRAIGHT FROM POTA

pota.app no longer has a hunted-parks CSV, so this little bookmark asks POTA for your list while you're signed in there, then brings just the park numbers back here. Your POTA password and sign-in never leave pota.app.

  1. On a computer: drag this button up to your bookmarks bar: ⭐ KPC: MY POTA PARKS
  2. On a phone: tap , bookmark any page, then edit that bookmark: name it KPC parks and paste over its web address.
  3. Go to pota.app and sign in. Then tap the bookmark (on Android Chrome: type KPC parks in the address bar and pick the bookmark).
  4. You land back here with your parks loaded. Do it again any time to refresh.

Or load a file:

📄 TAP TO PICK YOUR FILEor drop it here · CSV or ADIF · POTA, WWFF and SOTA

Where do I get the file?

  • POTA: use the ⭐ bookmark on the left (pota.app's own CSV download is for activations only). Or an ADIF from your logging program or your QRZ Logbook: the page reads the park numbers in it.
  • WWFF / KFF: your log from WWFF Logsearch, or any ADIF from your logger with WWFF numbers in it.
  • SOTA: sign in at sotadata.org.uk and download your chaser log as a CSV file.
  • Any CSV or ADIF works: the page just looks for park, reference and summit numbers in it. Old POTA numbers like K-1234 count as US-1234.
Will it remember me? Yes, on this phone or computer. Your list is saved in this web browser, not on GitHub or anywhere else, so it's still here when you come back in 4 days (or 4 months). It's gone if you clear your browser's data or use a private window, and a different phone needs its own upload. Your log only changes when you upload again, so do that every week or two. Tapping ✓ WORKED on a spot adds that park right away.
