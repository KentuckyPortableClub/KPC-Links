🚨 **THIS CHANNEL IS LIVE.** Every post here goes public within about 5 minutes. No chatting, no tests, nothing private.

**HOW IT WORKS**
Put a **tag** at the very start of your post, end it with a **colon ( : )**, then write your post.
> `Facebook + Discord: Gate opens at noon Friday`
No tag = **Website + Facebook + Discord**.
The tag is removed before posting. Words in the middle of a sentence never send anything.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
**📍 WHERE IT GOES**

`Website:`  →  website (+ the other Discord)

`Facebook:`  →  Facebook only

`Discord:`  →  the other Discord server only

`Groups.io:`  →  Groups.io only

`Subscribers:`  →  email-updates list, right away

`Email:`  →  Groups.io now + subscribers on Sunday

📧 Subscribers pick what they get: **Everything**, **Campout only** (only posts that mention the campout) or **Sunday roundup only** (one email a week with everything).

`All:`  →  everywhere

Mix them in any order: `Facebook + Groups.io:`  `FB+Discord:`

**➖ EVERYWHERE EXCEPT…** (put a minus or "No" in front)
`-Groups.io:`  →  everywhere except Groups.io
`No Facebook:`  →  everywhere except Facebook
`All except Email:`  →  everywhere, no email
