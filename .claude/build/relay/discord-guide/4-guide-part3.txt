**📝 NOTE (NOT shared anywhere)**

`Skip: this is just a note`
`Ignore:` and `Do not share:` work the same way.
Must be the very first word, with the colon.
Edit a shared post to start with `Skip:` → it comes down everywhere (not email).
Removing `Skip:` later won't share it. Post it again instead.

**✏️ EDIT • 🗑️ DELETE • 📌 PIN**

Edit your post → website, Facebook and Discord update.
Delete your post → removed from all of them.
Pin your post → stays at the top of its website page.
⚠️ Email can NOT be unsent or changed once it goes out.
