ABOUT THIS GUIDE

All 216 SOTA summits in association W4K (Kentucky), from the SOTLAS summit export. Each summit code opens its SOTLAS page. Points and elevation come from that export. Cards also list POTA and WWFF (KFF) parks within 3 miles.

On this page the summit list comes from the club’s live Kentucky data file, so it stays current.

FIELD-USE NOTE

Many summits are on private land — get permission. Operate within the SOTA activation zone and follow the SOTA General Rules and W4K Association Reference Manual. Verify access, closures and hunting seasons before travel.

Access: Permission & Safety First

Many Kentucky summits are on private, mining or timber land. Always get landowner permission where needed, and check SOTLAS and the W4K reference manual for access notes before you go.

This guide is a research tool, not the final word — do your homework before you go and confirm you are on the correct summit and inside its activation zone.

HOW TO READ EACH CARD

W4K/XX-###
Opens the summit’s SOTLAS page (details, photos, activations).
POTA / KFF WITHIN 3 MI
Parks near the summit — distance to the park reference point; check boundaries.
INSIDE PUBLIC LAND (KY GIS)
The summit point falls inside an official Kentucky public-land polygon; confirm before claiming.
GPS
The summit’s GPS point in Google Maps, for directions.
OSM MAP
OpenStreetMap at the summit — community-edited data.
WEATHER
The Weather Channel forecast for the summit’s GPS point.
NEARBY
Jumps to this summit’s nearby-summits list (nearest + all within 10 mi).
