HOW IT WORKS

Post in about a minute

  • Tap POST MY ACTIVATION or REQUEST A PARK. It opens a short Google Form: your call sign, then mostly tap-to-pick answers.
  • Park, summit and reference numbers only (like US-1277, KFF-1277 or W4K/EC-001). Two or more? Put commas between.
  • Your post shows here within about 20 minutes. Only your call sign is shown, never your name or email.
  • Posts are checked automatically: a real licensed call sign, real park numbers, a date in the next 60 days, and notes without links, phone numbers or bad words. Anything else waits for a club officer.
  • Past activations drop off by themselves. Park requests stay up for 30 days.
  • This board is for planning. To tell people you're on the air right now, also spot yourself with Spot Me.
