**Official sources found (state open-data portal = arcgis.water.nv.gov, Nevada Division of Water Resources / State Lands host):**
1. **NDSP_State_Park_Bondaries** FeatureServer (Nevada Division of State Parks' own park boundaries) = the answer for the real parks: US-2638 Van Sickle Bi-State, US-4438 Buckland Station (and the pin-inside parks that are state parks). Step 2 (agency's own record).
2. **SCORPRecAreas_Master** FeatureServer (state recreation inventory) and **NDSL / Nevada_State_Lands** + **NDSL_INTERESTS** MapServers (State Lands: ownership and interests): candidates for fish hatcheries (US-13240 Gallagher, US-13242 Spring Creek Rearing Station), Artesia Lake WA (US-13023) and historic sites; step 4b ownership.
3. **Walking Box Ranch (US-13183)**: BLM-managed historic site, Clark County; the BLM's own surface-management layer is the boundary (BLM ARCGIS), not a state layer.
**The 55 "State Historical Monument" parks (US-13274 to US-13481: Austin, Tonopah, Reno, Hazen, Eureka, Silver Peak, the Camel Corps, etc.):** these are Nevada's roadside historical MARKERS (NSHM numbers). The official list is the State Historic Preservation Office: shpo.nv.gov "Markers Sorted by Number" / "by County" PDFs (marker text and location, no boundary). A marker is a plaque beside a road; the state owns no land around it. Their POTA pages are third-party (destination4x4, southwestexplorers, Wikipedia), no agency map.
