# GAP LAYERS: official map services for parks with no outline (Research desk, chat 8, Mon Oct 26 ~10 PM)

Mission (HQ, 9:26 PM): for the 10 US states and 3 Canadian provinces with the most outline-less parks, find official, free, public map services not yet read. The desk edited no data file and no page.

## How the lists were made (and the limits)
- **Source of "no outline":** `boundary-score.json` is not in the repo (it only lives in the nightly issue body). So the desk counted from the answer keys, `data/us-key/US-XX.json` and `data/ca-key/CA-XX.json`: a park with no joined outline (`m` empty). Totals before filtering: US 2,759, Canada about 1,300.
- **Single spots and lines left out:** names or kinds with historic site, trailhead, dam, boat ramp, landing, lighthouse, memorial, monument, fort, airstrip, harbor, scenic byway/parkway, trail. That leaves 965 area-type parks in the US keys (the filter is rough; for example Nevada's nine "Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty" are scenic roads, so Nevada is really about zero).
- **"Already read":** the desk compared against `state-gis.json`, `us-key/layers.json`, `ca-key/layers.json` and `access-layers`, `campstate-layers`, `trails-layers` (grep for each service name), plus chat 2's `data/outline-leads/tested.json`.
- **What the desk could open:** only pages that search surfaced, through a fetch tool that reads page text. It cannot run a query against any map service. So each find has an evidence grade: **A** = the service's own page/metadata was opened (layers or fields seen); **B** = the service or hub exists (search result, partly opened) but its layers or fields were not seen; **C** = named from search title only. **No find has been test-queried.** The cheapest next step is chat 2's lead tester (`kpc_outline_lead_test.py`) on the links below; it counts how many of our outline-less parks each one really gives.
- **Keys and logins:** every link below is a public address with no key or token (the desk saw none asked for).

## 3. BIGGER WINS THAN NEW LAYERS: layers already read that do not join
These parks are outline-less although the right agency layer is already in the keys. That points at name matching, not at missing data. Chat 2 should check the names before searching for more.
- **BLM herd management areas** (layer `hma` is read): 15 parks still open (UT 8: Bonanza, Canyonlands, Robbers Roost, Onaqui, Conger, Sulphur...; WY 5; NV 1; ID 1). **BLM recreation management areas** (`rma`, `rst`): 30 special recreation management areas still open (UT 10, CA 5, WY 3, OR 3...). **BLM wilderness** (`WLD_WSA` is read): UT 6 wilderness areas still open. POTA names carry "BLM Herd Management Area" / "BLM Wilderness Area"; worth looking at why they fail.
- **New Brunswick:** the wildlife refuge/WMA layer (`gis-erd-der.gnb.ca/.../OpenData/WildlifeRefuge`) is in ca-key, yet 24 WMAs remain, all named "New Brunswick #24 Wildlife Management Area", "#19"... A number join may fix all 24. Provincial protected natural areas (PNAs, Nature Legacy) are also read, yet 15 "Nature Reserve" and 10 "Ecological Area" parks remain.
- **Michigan:** `DNRWILDLandsOPENDATA` (state game areas) is read ("matched 23 of 52") yet 16 "State Game Land" parks remain (Norwich, Blue Bill Creek, Cedar River...). Michigan's other layers on the same server (https://services3.arcgis.com/Jdnp1TjADvSDxMAX/ArcGIS/rest/services/DNRManagementBoundariesOPENDATA/FeatureServer) are Parks Unit (read), State Forest management units (layer 2; 6 state forests open), Trails management units, and no layer for game areas or harbors. The 13 state harbors are points.
- **Ontario:** conservation authorities already read (TRCA, NPCA, GRCA, ABCA, ERCA, KRCA, CVC, LSRCA, others) still show Bond Lake, Glassco Park, East and West Duffins Headwaters, Bathurst Glen (all Toronto region) and Decew Falls (Niagara) as open: name mismatch is likely.
- **Oklahoma:** WMA layer (`odw`) is read; four WMAs open (Lake Carl Blackwell, Selman, Camp Gruber, Sparrow Hawk). Lake Carl Blackwell (Oklahoma State University) and Camp Gruber (National Guard) are not ODWC-managed, so no ODWC layer will ever show them.
- **Arkansas:** AGFC fishing-water and WMA layers sit in `tested.json` with 1-2 parks each, not added. Blanchard Springs and White Rock Mountain are Forest Service recreation areas.
- **Utah, Washington, Oregon:** the park, WMA and state-land layers are read. Only the BLM, state forest and W&S river items above are open.

## 4. WHAT WAS NOT WORTH CHASING (single spots and lines left in the "no outline" counts)
Idaho's 30 backcountry airstrips; Michigan's 13 state harbors; the "State Trail" and "National Parkway" entries (scenic byways, water trails: lines); Nevada's nine scenic roads; Mississippi's 15 state fishing lakes and 21 Corps-lake recreation areas (no polygon source found); the 40 or so one-off municipal parks per province. National Wild and Scenic Rivers (51 parks in the US keys; Michigan 16, Alaska 9, Oregon 7): the NPS corridors are already read; the Forest Service layer https://apps.fs.usda.gov/ArcX/rest/services/EDW/EDW_WildScenicRiverSegments_01/MapServer/0 is titled "National Wild and Scenic River Line" (center lines), which would not give an outline.
