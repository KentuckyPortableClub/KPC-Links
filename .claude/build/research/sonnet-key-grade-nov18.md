## Patterns

1. **Fragment adds (about 740 parks: MA 354, MO 247, WI 72, CO 31 ...), RIGHT.** Same exact-name pieces from a second layer (sgmad MassGIS, mol Missouri, etc.) joined to a park that already had them; 'ac' unchanged (median ratio 1.00). Harmless, but see pattern 5.
2. **Contest (RIGHT, working).** About 150 removals where a record named for a sister park (SP vs SF vs WA vs SWA vs NWR) went to the better fit. Examples: Ohio SP/SF/WA pairs (Pike Lake SP lost 'Pike SF'), Maryland (Elk Neck SP vs SF), Nebraska SHP vs SRA (Fort Kearny). No misfire seen except pattern 3.
3. **WRONG kind: contest/R-G8 reading words too literally (3 rows).** (a) Contest: 'State Reservation' vs 'State Reserve' not seen as the same kind: MA US-8417 Mount Tom lost all its own pieces (also watch other MA 'Reservation' parks: US-11476 is fine). (b) R-G8: 'Autauga County' inside the park's full name: AL US-3679 Kelley WMA lost its 10,465-ac record. Watch for the same on other 'X County WMA' names (AL US-3680, US-9919 correctly lost 'County Public Fishing Lake' records, so only the full-name case misfires). (c) Trail park: MN US-9386 lost its trail line for 3-ac same-name pieces.
4. **Bare-name ties (27 UNSURE, mostly Ohio, 25).** Ohio ODNR layer names a SP, SF, WA and nature preserve the same bare word (SHAWNEE, CAESAR CREEK, SALT FORK). Tie rule sends the big record to nobody. Correct by the rule, but the busiest parks (Caesar Creek SP 1,999, Salt Fork, Shawnee) now rely on fewer records; a person should give each bare record to its park. Examples: Caesar Creek SP (CAESAR CREEK 6,806 ac), Delaware SP, Shawnee SP.
5. **'ac' not trustworthy where duplicate layers join (3 UNSURE, MA).** Savoy Mountain SF 13,373 -> 49,593, Harold Parker 3,376 -> 10,863, Mount Holyoke Range 5,138 -> 19,811: acres summed over duplicate sgmad+sgmaa pieces.
