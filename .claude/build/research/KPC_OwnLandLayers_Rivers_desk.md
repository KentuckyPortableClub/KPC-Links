## 2. The BOT TEST (one pass, chat 2 / chat 9 bot; ~10 queries)
For each point below, run `query?geometry=<lon,lat>&geometryType=esriGeometryPoint&inSR=4326&outFields=*&returnGeometry=false&f=json` on the layer and write: HIT (owner/interest/status) or NO HIT. A NO HIT in the middle of a river that sits inside the unit = "the layer cuts the river out".
- NPS tracts: Green River in Mammoth Cave (37.1856, -86.1305: Brandon's case, check three points along the river), New River Gorge (37.9250, -81.0780), Big South Fork (36.4950, -84.6930).
- FWS full interest layer: Ohio River Islands NWR channel (39.3300, -81.7000 on the Ohio River; take a point mid-channel from the NWR polygon), Cache River NWR (Arkansas), a Michigan Detroit River NWR water point. Record INTTYPE1 (O = fee) for each.
- Forest Service Basic Ownership: Daniel Boone NF Red River gorge channel (37.8200, -83.6300), Cumberland River inside DBNF (36.8400, -84.3200).
- Ohio DNR: Mosquito Lake mid-lake (41.3000, -80.7600) in `Lands_Public_nolakes` vs `ODNR_Lands_Dissolved` (does the second one hit?).
- Michigan: a point in Lake Michigan inside Warren Dunes (41.9150, -86.5950); Manistee River inside Manistee NF.
Pass rule: a layer whose NO HIT rate on in-unit water is > 0 is "cuts rivers out": its rows can place a park's LAND only; the river slot must come from the agency's own boundary / the corridor polygon, and any 'gap' it creates inside a corridor is NOT a real other owner unless a land record names one (Brandon's check).

## 3. Reading order for chat 2's "agency's OWN land records first" (proposal, grade C until the test is back)
1. NPS tracts (park units). 2. FWS interest, fee only (refuges). 3. Forest Service Basic Ownership (forests; add the wilderness / W&S corridor polygons the agency publishes). 4. State agency lands WITH water (OH "External3 / Dissolved", MI DNR open data). 5. PAD-US last (we saw it cut the Green River out).
Rule to add: whichever layer is used, a **water gap between two land parcels of the SAME owner is not a gap** (the river is inside the same park); only a gap that a land record gives to a DIFFERENT owner, or a USGS water body the agency does not claim (navigable bed owned by the state), counts.
