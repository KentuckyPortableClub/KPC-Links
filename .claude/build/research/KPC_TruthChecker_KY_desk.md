## What the checker needs fixed (for chat 2 / chat 10)
1. **Page bleed:** when one page covers several units (refuge complexes), read the acreage sentence for THIS unit only (3 of 11 flags). 
2. **History numbers:** ignore 'land grant', 'patent', 'originally' sentences and require 'acres' within 100 characters of 'currently / total / consists / encompasses / size'.
3. **Slices and containers:** if the park spans states or the agency number is for a container ('included in the 125,000-acre NRRA'), do not flag; list as 'compare by hand'.
4. **Match designation, not name:** state Wild River vs National W&S River share a name; the checker must see 'State' vs 'National' in the type.
5. **Units:** it prints 'agency 21,380 hectares' next to ours in acres; convert and show both.
6. **Mark each flag with its cause class** (our union / boundary vs owned / checker page / designation) so Brandon sees five, not eleven.
