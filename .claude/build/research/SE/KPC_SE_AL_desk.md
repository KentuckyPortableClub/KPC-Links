## 4. Tricky land
- **WMAs on TVA land.** Wikipedia: Lauderdale WMA is on TVA land managed by the state (B); the TVA reservoir "habitat" tracts named in TVA NEPA notices (e.g. Raccoon Creek Waterfowl Pond) sit on TVA land (B). Expect North Alabama WMAs (Lauderdale, Guntersville-area, Wheeler?) on TVA/Corps/refuge land: ADCNR layers show them as WMAs but the owner is TVA → rule 14.
- **Corps WMAs:** Black Warrior and Tombigbee (Corps hunting areas), Walter F. George / Eufaula Lake (B).
- **Forever Wild tracts:** state-owned (A: alabamaforeverwild.com), public. Some tracts are open for hunting under WMA rules and also carry a POTA number: overlap with WMA likely.
- **National forests:** Bankhead, Talladega, Conecuh, Tuskegee NFs (USFS): state WMAs inside (Choccolocco, Oakmulgee) are cooperative on USFS land (C, verify).
- Water-only: Mobile-Tensaw Delta WMA (partly water).
