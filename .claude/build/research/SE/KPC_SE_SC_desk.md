## 4. Tricky land
- **WMAs on Corps and national forest land.** SCDNR runs WMAs on Francis Marion and Sumter NFs (USFS land under cooperative agreement) and on Corps lakes (Thurmond, Hartwell, Russell). Public Lands Viewer shows these as WMAs (B).
- **Private timber leases:** many SC WMA tracts are leased from timber companies; they are WMA by agreement (C).
- **Heritage preserves:** some are state-owned, some are easements.
- **Water-only:** coastal marsh WMAs (Santee Delta, Bear Island, ACE Basin).
