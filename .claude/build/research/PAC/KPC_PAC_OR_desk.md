## 3. Trails and access
Boating access sites, OPRD recreation facilities and sites read.

## 4. Tricky land
- **ODF state forests are managed for timber** (county trust lands); public access, but also private land.
- **Oregon Coast Trail** runs mostly on state beaches (public beach = "ocean shore" state recreation area, a corridor).
- Bureau of Reclamation/ Corps lakes with state parks (Detroit Lake, Cottage Grove).
