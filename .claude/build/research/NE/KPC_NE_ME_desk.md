# Northeast Source Book: MAINE (desk, Oct 27 2026)
Key: 148 parks, 12 unjoined; ONE layer (sgmea, Maine conserved lands, services1.arcgis.com/RbMX0mRVOFNTdLzd).

## 1. Official boundaries
Maine conserved lands (BPL / IFW / MNAP) cover state parks, public reserved lands, WMAs, ecological reserves in one layer. Maine DACF publishes it with the MNAP conservation lands list (B: maine.gov/dacf/mnap/assistance/conslands.htm). IFW Wildlife Management Districts are admin zones, not land (A: WMD FeatureServer exists).
