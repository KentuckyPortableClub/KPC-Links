## FIX BEFORE RELEASE (owner: chat 1, pages)
1. **Red River Gorge camping (Rules You Should Know, Daniel Boone card).** Page says "Back-country camping in the Red River Gorge is free with no permit". The Forest Service Red River Gorge page (opened) says a recreation permit must be displayed for backcountry camping 10 PM to 6 AM: $5/day, $7/3 days or $50/year per vehicle, sold at Forest offices and local vendors. The parking-pass line above it is fine. Source: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/danielboone/recreation/red-river-gorge (opened once through the fetch tool; worth one look by a person before the line is rewritten).
2. **Real call sign used as an example (house rule).** New to POTA: the sample QSO uses KZ4CP twice plus "Kilo Zulu Four Charlie Papa", and the hunter call KQ4HZK has a real-call format. New to WWFF: the log file example `KZ4CP@KFF-1234 20261013.adi`. Use K4TEST for the activator and a 1x4 call (W4TEST) for the hunter. (finder.html footer credit "Provided by KZ4CP" is a credit, not an example: left alone.)
3. **WWFF log file name (New to WWFF).** Page: `CALLSIGN@KFF-1234 YYYYMMDD.adi` (with a space). WWFF Global Rules v5.10 section 6.8 (opened) says `callsign@referenceYYYYMMDD` and gives no example. NOT SURE whether the KFF log managers want the space: check the KFF FAQ (https://wwff.us/?page_id=133) or ask a log manager, or print the rule's wording.
4. **"KFF also excludes satellites" (Rules You Should Know + New to WWFF).** The WWFF Global Rules v5.10 (opened) accept satellite contacts (section 3.10; 5.10 clarified it). A stricter KFF-only rule is not confirmed (the KFF FAQ could not be opened). NOT SURE: confirm at the KFF FAQ or drop the sentence.
5. **Nature preserves wording (Rules You Should Know).** Page: "prohibit audio equipment and electronic noise devices". The regulation, 400 KAR 2:090 (opened), prohibits "using audio equipment" at all times except approved research; "electronic noise devices" is not in it, and nothing mentions radios or headphones. Suggest: "Audio equipment is not allowed on nature preserves (400 KAR 2:090). A radio may count: ask the Office of Nature Preserves before you operate." Pets, camping, picnics, fires, bikes, horses, sunrise to sunset: all match (regulation + Quiet Trails brochure, opened).
6. **UTC day change (New to SOTA).** "Crossing 00:00 UTC (7 or 8 PM in Kentucky)" is right only for Eastern Kentucky. Central Kentucky is 6 or 7 PM. New to POTA and the POTA Room already give both zones: use the same wording.

## CONFIRMED against the official source (opened)
- KDFWR Public Lands Hunting Guide: group of 10 or more applies 30 days ahead; solid hunter orange head, back and chest in gun seasons (page says firearm deer, elk and bear: the guide lists modern gun, muzzleloader and youth gun deer, so check the elk and bear part); Peabody WMA permit ages 16+; Otter Creek permit; no nails, spikes, screw-ins or wire in trees on WMAs, Otter Creek and Daniel Boone NF; camping only in designated areas.
- Kentucky State Parks trail guidelines: sunrise to sunset, leashed pets, no pets on preserve trails (Natural Bridge except Henson Arch and Whittleton; McCreary side of Cumberland Falls), no alcohol on trails, no carving.
- WWFF Global Rules v5.10: 44 QSOs that add up over visits, fewer still count for hunters, whole station inside, one reference at a time, vehicles OK, any power, no repeaters / IRLP / Echolink / remote, log goes to the national coordinator or log manager (no self-upload).
- Band Plan: every segment and the power limits match 47 CFR 97.305 and the ARRL band chart as the desk knows them (the linked pages were not opened, so this one is from knowledge, not a fresh read); popular FT8 / FT4 / calling frequencies are the usual ones.
- Radio Conditions: Kp / G scale, R / S / G, X-ray classes, glossary and the NOAA links are right.
- UTC day tables (New to POTA, POTA Room): 8/7 PM Eastern, 7/6 PM Central: right.
