## Mexico (MX)
1. **Official layers:** CONANP "Información Geoespacial de las Áreas Naturales Protegidas": **SHAPE_ANPS.zip, shapefile polygons from the decree texts, annual, reference date 2022-07-27, metadata 2022-11-10, 185 federal ANP; no WMS/WFS; licence and use restrictions blank**; download page http://sig.conanp.gob.mx/website/pagsig/ (A: snieg.mx PDF). An older KML version (2019) is on datamx.io, "no licence provided" (A). State and municipal ANPs are NOT in it. 47 "State Park" + 12 "Natural Reserve" etc. need each state's own layer (none searched, B).
2. **Public/owned land:** **weak.** Federal ANPs in Mexico sit largely on **ejido/communal (social property) and private land**; the decree protects the land but ownership stays (C, well known). No national ownership polygon found (B). So federal-park pairs are the "whole-line over private land" case (W-2) unless INDAABIN/CONANP federal-property parcels are found. Marine/island parks are federal zone (ZOFEMAT) = public (C).
3. **No outline:** all 534. CONANP file would cover roughly the 94 National Parks + 37 Biosphere + 35 Fauna reserves (≈ 190 of them). The 175 archaeological reserves, 39 historic trails (lines), and state parks have no source.
4. **Sea/lake:** Cabo Pulmo MX-0003 (marine), Arrecife Alacranes, Revillagigedo, Cozumel reefs, Lake Chapala/Pátzcuaro: water-only.
5. **NEW grey areas:** X3-1: **federal ANP on ejido/private land** (W-2 whole-line). X3-2: **CONANP file licence blank** (ask CONANP; Mexican federal data is usually "Libre Uso MX", C).
