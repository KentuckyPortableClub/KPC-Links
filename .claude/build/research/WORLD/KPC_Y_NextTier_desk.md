## South Africa (ZA, 142 parks: Nature Reserve 69, National Park 27)
1. **Layers:** SANBI/SANParks NPAES "Formal Protected Areas" (custodian SANParks, distributor SANBI; 2010, begin date 2016-01-01; **copyright, "other restrictions", no licence named**) (A: https://metadata.sanbi.org/srv/api/records/d77b4a64-a147-42de-8ed4-aa08157c80b6). DFFE's SAPAD (protected areas) served through the Western Cape GIS (B: https://gis.westerncape.gov.za/server2/rest/services/SpatialDataWarehouse/DFFE_ConservationSites/MapServer/0) and DPME (DFFE2022Q1, B). 2. **Public land:** SANParks are state land; many nature reserves are municipal or private. 3. **Work: small-medium; licence to ask.** Grey Z-1: private game reserves in the reserves list (private by agreement; keep out).
## Argentina (AR, 389 parks: Nature Reserve 93, National Park 45, Nature Park 41, Park Reserve 37, Provincial Park 25, Biosphere Reserve 14, Natura 2000 11 [sic])
1. **Layers:** the national page for protected areas / SIFAP gives **no shapefile, KMZ or WMS** and no licence (A: argentina.gob.ar/ambiente/areas-protegidas); the national parks administration (APN) and IGN publish layers elsewhere (not found). Registration in SIFAP is voluntary, provinces differ (A). 2. Public land: national parks are federal; provincial reserves often include private estancias. 3. **NOT READY; work medium-large (24 provinces).**
## Philippines (PH, 264 parks: National Park 73, Plaza Park 15, Ecological Park 7...)
1. **Layers:** a WCS-hosted feature service "National Integrated Protected Areas System" (248 areas, 7.97 M ha; sources DENR-BMB/NAMRIA named under Access Information) with the licence line **"Shall not be used for any legal purposes"** and no date (A: https://gisportal.wcs.org/arcgis/rest/services/Hosted/National_Integrated_Protected_Areas_System/FeatureServer/1). That is a **warning, not an open licence.** 2. Public land: NIPAS areas are state land, with IP ancestral domains and private claims inside (C). 3. **NOT READY** until DENR-BMB gives a file with terms. Grey P-1: plazas and city parks (never).
