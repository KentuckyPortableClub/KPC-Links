Grades: A = opened and read, B = search result only, C = inference. Read only; no polygon queried. Builds on KPC_2fer_Preflight_World_desk.md (W-1..W-8). WDPA never. Park counts/types are from data/pota (our POTA park list); these countries are NOT in data/world-key (no outlines yet).
Parks: 200 (National Park 34, National Monument 31, Natural Monument 29, Wildlife Refuge 22, Protected Landscape 17, Forest Reserve 15, Recreation Area 9). Past the top 10: Brandon must lift the "no new boundaries" rule for DO.
1. **Official layers:** Ministerio de Medio Ambiente y Recursos Naturales publishes KMZ files at https://ambiente.gob.do/sinap-kmz/ (A): Sistema Nacional de Áreas Protegidas (act. Feb 2026, dated 11 Mar 2026), Zona de Amortiguamiento (buffer zones), Áreas Protegidas Metropolitanas, Cinturón Verde. **Format KMZ only; no licence named; site footer "Todos los derechos reservados"** (A). The national statistics office ONE / datos.gob.do "Áreas Protegidas en República Dominicana 2007-2024" is **ODbL, updated 29 Sep 2025, but CSV/ODS/XLSX only (percentages, no shapes)** (A: https://datos.gob.do/dataset/areas-protegidas-en-republica-dominicana). A 2025 ONE PDF "Caracterización de Áreas Protegidas Terrestres" exists (B, not opened). Legal base: Ley 202-04.
2. **Public/owned land:** by Ley 202-04 the national system is state land, but the news (A, search headlines: eldia.com.do, elcaribe.com.do, poderjudicial.gob.do) says **many protected areas still have no land title; the government and the courts are titling them now** (757 million m² titled so far). Some parcels inside are private or squatted. So national parks are public by law (C) but "owned" is partly paper. No parcel layer. Lean: public by designation; mark private-by-agreement areas (the first private protected area in the south was declared recently, B) as out.
3. **Parks with no outline:** all 200. Best cover: the SINAP KMZ (should hold every system area; name match needed: POTA names are English suffixes like "Natural Monument", the KMZ is Spanish "Monumento Natural"). Small monuments (waterfalls, hills, e.g. DO-0070 Salto Grande, DO-0059 Loma La Altagracia) may be points only in the KMZ (C).
4. **Sea/lake:** Isla Catalina DO-0051 (island + reef), Saona, Cayos Siete Hermanos, Humpback whale sanctuary: water-only; Lago Enriquillo (salt lake, hot).
5. **NEW grey areas:** X1-1: **KMZ with "all rights reserved" and no licence**: use needs the Ministry's OK (ask; same as Mexico). X1-2: **untitled protected areas**: do we call state-by-designation land "public"? Lean: yes for national parks and monuments, NOT SURE for buffer zones (Zona de Amortiguamiento is private land). X1-3: **buffer zones must never count as park**.
