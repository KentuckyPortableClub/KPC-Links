# STAGE 3: NEW ZEALAND (NZ) (desk, Oct 27 2026)
Grades: A = opened and read, B = search result only, C = inference. Read only; no polygon queried. WDPA never. Park counts/types from data/pota (our POTA list). Country is NOT in data/world-key.
Parks: 893 (Conservation Area 338, Scenic Reserve 172, Ecological Area 84, Regional Park 45, Conservation Park 43, Marine Reserve 40, Nature Reserve 18, plus National Parks, Amenity Areas, Wildlife areas). **Best country in the world for this project: about 90 % of the list is DOC public conservation land.**
1. **Official layers:** DOC Public Conservation Land, ArcGIS FeatureServer https://services1.arcgis.com/3JjYDyG3oajxU6HO/ArcGIS/rest/services/DOC_Public_Conservation_Land/FeatureServer/0 (A). Polygons from NaPALIS; fields **Type** (Conservation Area, Marginal Strip, Marine Area...), **Section** (65+ legal categories, e.g. s.20 Wilderness Area), Legislation, Name, NaPALIS_ID, Recorded_Area, Start/End date, flags (Vested, Control_Managed, Private_Ownership, Classified). Main categories: National Park, Conservation Park, Specially Protected Area, Reserve, Stewardship Area, Marginal Strip, Wildlife Management Area. **Last edit 12 Oct 2026** (A). Also on data.govt.nz: https://catalogue.data.govt.nz/dataset/doc-public-conservation-land5 (listed; page blocked by a firewall, B). Other DOC layers: hunting permit areas, huts, tracks (B). DOC also runs an open-data portal https://doc-deptconservation.opendata.arcgis.com/ (A, linked from DOC terms).
2. **Licence (probe done, W-7): CC BY 4.0, copyright text "Department of Conservation"; credit "Crown Copyright: Department of Conservation Te Papa Atawhai [year]"; state the acquisition date and that it may be out of date** (A: layer page + https://www.doc.govt.nz/our-work/maps-and-data/terms-and-conditions/). DOC says it is moving from CC BY 3.0 NZ to CC BY 4.0 "where appropriate"; check each item's metadata; commercial use not addressed. KPC is non-commercial: OK. **The layer page itself says CC BY 4.0.**
3. **Public/owned land (rule 14):** **the DOC layer is the owner data**: every polygon is Crown conservation land (public). A **Private_Ownership flag** and "Overlays" show where the line includes land not DOC-owned (A). LINZ parcels/titles are on the LINZ Data Service; **bulk ownership needs a LINZ Licence for Personal Data** (A), so do not use LINZ owner names; LINZ Managed Crown Property is also on data.govt.nz (B). **Regional Parks (45) are regional-council land** (public, Auckland Council, Greater Wellington...): each council has its own layer (B: several council ArcGIS servers surfaced), not DOC. **Marine Reserves (40) are DOC too but water-only.**
4. **No outline:** all 893. DOC layer covers ≈ 800 (conservation areas, scenic/ecological/nature reserves, parks, marginal strips); gaps: 45 Regional Parks (council layers), local-authority reserves, and names: POTA uses DOC names plus type words ("Kepler Mire Conservation Area") (name mismatch likely small since DOC names are the same).
5. **Sea/lake:** 40 Marine Reserves (Poor Knights, Cape Rodney–Okakari Point) and lake beds: water-only list. Lake Taupō etc.: bed is Crown, but water-only.
6. **NEW grey areas:** Y1-1: **Marginal Strips** (20 m public strips along rivers/lakes) as parks or as part of larger ones: lean not their own park, they are not in POTA's list as a category (C). Y1-2: **Stewardship Areas and "Conservation Area" vs "Conservation Park"**: all public; 2-fers between **a Conservation Park and a Scenic Reserve inside it** are the common NZ overlap; they are layered, true overlaps in the DOC layer (**layered = real, rule 14 passes by design**). Y1-3: **Private_Ownership = yes inside a DOC polygon** (covenants, leases): exclude those pieces. Y1-4: **Māori-owned land managed by DOC (Whenua Rahui, s.58)**: private by agreement, keep out (rule "private-by-agreement"). Y1-5: **Regional Parks in two councils' layers** may overlap DOC land (shared boundaries): check per pair.
7. **Known-good / 2-fers (C):** Fiordland NP with Fiordland (Te Wāhipounamu) World Heritage; Kahurangi NP; Abel Tasman NP (Tonga Island Marine Reserve is a 2-fer with water-only parts); Tongariro NP (dual WHS); Rakiura NP; Egmont/Taranaki NP; a scenic reserve inside a conservation park (many). Expect many.
8. **READY (outlines + owner data): YES, after adding the DOC layer and the Regional Park layers.** Missing: Regional Park layers per council (45 parks, ~10 councils), name pass. **Work: small to medium (one layer for ~800 parks; DOC names are already POTA's). Easiest big country; recommended first pick.**
