1. **Official layers:** data.go.kr (the national open-data portal): **"한국보호지역 데이터" (Korea Protected Areas) from the National Park Service Corporation: shapefile, protected areas under 16 laws and 5 ministries, 30 types, 64,394 sites; dated 2016-12-31, registered 2024-05-03, last modified 2025-04-24; "이용허락범위 제한 없음" (no restriction on use), free** (A: https://www.data.go.kr/data/15127921/fileData.do). The page does not say outright that they are boundary polygons (B, likely). Also 국립공원공단 datasets: national park facilities, scenic resources (2025-04-28), linear facilities (2018) (B). A third-party blog explains downloading the park SHP from KDPA (B). Local governments publish thematic maps: e.g. Hamyang-gun Natural Park Zone/District continuous thematic maps (SHP, registered 2026-05-07, "no restrictions") from **KLIP** (land-use platform) (A: data.go.kr/data/15159365 and 15159364) = proof that the **zoning (natural park zone / district) layers exist nationwide through KLIP**. Urban parks: "전국도시공원정보표준데이터" (national urban park info standard data, B), likely points with area, not outlines.
2. **Public/owned land:** no ownership layer found. **Korea Forest Service (산림청) manages state forest and the national recreation forests** (자연휴양림, e.g. KR-0231 Jirisan Natural Recreation Forest); cadastral (지적) data with owner type is a national "연속지적도" but owner type is not open (C). National parks in Korea include **a lot of private land inside the boundary** (Natural Parks Act zoning applies to private land) (C, well known): W-2 whole-line case. **City parks (도시공원) are public land by definition** (C).
