## Greenland
1. **Layers:** Greenland's own GIS portal Nunagis (nunagis.gl) holds the official map layers (B: QGreenland lists a Nunagis closed-areas layer); the 12 protected areas are the Northeast Greenland NP, Melville Bay nature reserve, Ilulissat Icefjord and 9 others (A: aeco.no PDF, names only, no boundaries). Aarhus University (DCE) hosts a national-park layer on augis.au.dk (B, a report figure, not an official source). 11 of our 12 are Ramsar sites.
2. **Public land:** **all Greenland land is public** (no private land ownership; use rights by the Self-Government) (C, well known, not opened). That makes rule 14 easy, but nobody can use it: the sites are remote.
3. **No outline:** all 12. Cover: Nunagis if the licence allows.
