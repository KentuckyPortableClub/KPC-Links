# STAGE 2 X2: THE CARIBBEAN PACK (desk, Oct 27 2026)
Grades: A = opened and read, B = search result only, C = inference. Read only; no polygon queried. Builds on KPC_2fer_Preflight_World_desk.md (W-1..W-8). WDPA never. Park counts/types are from data/pota (our POTA park list); these countries are NOT in data/world-key (no outlines yet).
Pack: BB CU LC BM TT TC KY VC DM BS GP CW KN BQ AG AW VG AI GD JM BL SX MQ MF MS HT. Our list holds: CU 283, TT 147, BS 49, LC 47, BB 45, VC 36, GP 29, BM 28, MF 23, VG 21, DM 17, GD 16, JM 15, BQ 12, MQ 10, BL 9, AI 8, TC 7, CW 6, AG 5, KN 3, SX 3, MS 2, AW 1, KY 50 (note: POTA prefix KY here is Cayman, not Kentucky); HT has no parks yet. About 880 parks. Most are tiny islands, beaches, forts and historic sites.
## 1. Official layers, by group
- **French overseas (GP 29, MQ 10, MF 23, BL 9 = 71 parks):** the only group with a real national source. INPN (the French national nature inventory) downloads page for reserves lists **Guadeloupe (2012), Martinique (12/2019 + a protection-perimeter layer), Saint-Martin (2012), Saint-Barthélemy (2012)**, Mapinfo/Shape; **licence not named on the page ("freely downloadable subject to conditions of use")** (A: https://inpn.mnhn.fr/telechargement/cartes-et-information-geographique/ep/rnn). Guadeloupe National Park (parc national) and the Conservatoire du littoral lands: INPN INSPIRE record found (B). These are the SAME INPN family as Stage 1 France, so the French rules (ZNIEFF = NOT SURE, W-1) apply. Saint-Martin: also French/Dutch split on the island (SX is the Dutch half).
- **Cuba (CU 283):** SNAP (national system) maps exist; I found **no open boundary file** (B). NOAA's Gulf Data Atlas hosts a "Federal Protected Areas - Cuba" layer (B: https://gis.ngdc.noaa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/GulfDataAtlas/FederalProtectedAreas_Cuba/MapServer/0), likely coarse and derived; licence unread. 35 Historic Sites + 13 Archeological Reserves + battlefields are never.
- **Trinidad & Tobago (TT 147):** no national open layer found (B); 32 Historic Sites + 19 Heritage Sites are never; Forestry Division reserves are the main public land.
- **Jamaica (JM 15):** NEPA protected-areas branch PDFs only (B). **Bahamas (BS 49):** Bahamas National Trust runs the parks (a public-private trust): no layer found (B). **Barbados (BB 45), St Lucia (LC 47), St Vincent (VC 36), Dominica (DM 17), Grenada (GD 16), Antigua (AG), St Kitts (KN):** national parks/forest reserves, no open layer found (B).
- **UK territories (BM 28, KY 50, TC 7, VG 21, AI 8, MS 2):** no layer found (B; not searched territory by territory). Cayman's Department of Environment and Bermuda's Department of Parks are the owners to ask. **Dutch (BQ 12, CW 6, AW 1, SX 3):** only ProtectedPlanet pages surfaced (B; WDPA never). **Haiti (HT):** no parks in our list.
## 2. Public/owned land
Mostly **government or trust land** (forest reserves, national parks, Crown/State land), but **no parcel layer exists in any of them** (B/C). Dutch and UK islands have land registries but not open ones. So pairs are NOT SURE except where one agency owns both parks (C).
## 3. No outline
All ~880 (not in key). Best cover: INPN for the 71 French-island parks; a hand-made list for the rest. Realistic result: **a few islands' parks get pins only, never an outline.**
## 4. Sea/lake
Heavy: marine parks and reefs (Bonaire National Marine Park, Soufrière MMA, Folkestone, Exuma Cays Land and Sea Park BS, Tobago Cays VC, Buccoo Reef TT): water-only list. Many "beach park" patches are shoreline strips.
## 5. NEW grey areas
X2-1: **beach parks and shoreline strips** (e.g. BB-0035 Little Bay, TT-0135 No Man's Land Beach Park): public beach vs private shoreline; lean pin-only. X2-2: **Cuba "protected area" labels that are historic sites or battlefields** (CU-0458 Playa Girón): historic rule, never. X2-3: **two nations on one island** (Saint-Martin/Sint Maarten): different data sources, can't overlap across the border; keep apart. X2-4: **trust-run parks** (Bahamas National Trust, Barbados' Queens Park): treat as public if open daily.
