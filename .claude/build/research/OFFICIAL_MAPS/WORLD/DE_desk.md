# Germany (DE) official park maps (desk, Oct 28 2026)
Our key: 1,265 parks: Recreation Park 182; Nature Reserve 175; Flora and Fauna Reserve 156; Nature Park 112; Natura 2000 95; National Historic Trail 61; National Nature Reserve 41; Bird Sanctuary 39. World note: mostly private/mixed ownership (W-2).
Germany has no national park-map list: protected areas belong to the 16 Länder.
