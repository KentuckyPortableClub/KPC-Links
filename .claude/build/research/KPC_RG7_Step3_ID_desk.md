## GREY AREA G-ID1 (for Brandon, via HQ): airstrip parks
R-G7 step 4 draws "the land the park's agency owns". The Idaho airstrips are run by ITD Aeronautics but sit on Forest Service / BLM land under permits, so the agency (ITD) owns no land and there is no boundary line. Choices: (a) stay pin only; (b) the maintained runway strip from the permit (needs the permit text, a few have it); (c) the federal landowner's parcel is the park. Until ruled: all 20 stay pins. Same question will come up for airstrips in other states (BLM airstrips alone: 27 in the key).
