## FIXES
- **GA-R1 Date each SENTENCE, not the heading.** Grand Bay WMA has the gate-hours text (7/9/21) and 'The boardwalk is closed until further notice' (10/08/26) under the same heading. The sheet's example 3 says 'use the newest'; that wrongly stamps the old hours text as new. Rule: the closure sentence's date = the Updated line directly above THAT sentence; no Updated line (the 'general' entries) = 'dates not given'.
- **GA-R2 Negation traps (the page has one).** The shooting-range section says 'No shooting range closures at the moment.' It contains 'closures' but is the opposite of one. Same fix as the closures-bot D-R1: a closing word with no 'no / not / none / remains open / minimal impact' in the sentence, and a named object. 'Please note that the stream near Holly Creek Bridge is undergoing maintenance but should have minimal impact on traffic' (Region 1) = NOT a closure (no closing word); 'A timber buyer was accepted … Macedonia reforestation project' (Allatoona) = not a closure.
- **GA-R3 One sentence, two facts (Tuckahoe WMA, 8/5/26):** 'The bathhouse and north side of the campground will be closed for renovations starting July 15'. Split: bathhouse = a facility NOTE (G5); north side of the campground = PART CLOSED (campground, north side), south end stays open. 'July 15' has no year: take the year of the Updated date (2026) and the end 'January 2027' from the sentence. Both items carry the agency's whole sentence.
- **GA-R4 Intermittent and periodic closures.** Cohutta: 'Emery Creek Trailhead will experience intermittent closures from September 8 through the end of December' and 'FS Road 18 temporary closure beginning September 8' (paving through November). Grand Bay: 'Due to military training needs on the federally owned portion (Highway 221 tract) … periodically …'. Rule: 'intermittent / periodically / at times' = a '📅 SEASONAL / on and off' item with the window in the agency's words; NOT ⚠ CLOSED. A window that has ended (Nov 30 passed) drops off by date; today (Oct 28) Cohutta is still in force and must show. Grand Bay's military closure (2021, no dates, standing) = a quiet NOTE, since it is a standing condition, not an event.
- **GA-R5 Gate hours are hours, not closures.** 'The automatic gate to the Grand Bay WMA boardwalk and lookout tower is open from 6 a.m.-8 p.m. daily' = park INFO (hours, for the hours bot), never PART CLOSED. 'Review the gate openings and hunting season information' (Region 1) = a hunting note.
