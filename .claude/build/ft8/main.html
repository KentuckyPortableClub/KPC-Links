The dial is the standard frequency for this mode on this band. The offset is worked out from the spot, so it is only right when the spotter gave their exact frequency. Look for the call in your decode window.

💻 ON A COMPUTER (WSJT-X)

  1. Set WSJT-X to FT8 and the dial to — (Band box, or your radio).
  2. Wait for — to show up in the Band Activity list (one 15-second cycle, sometimes a few).
  3. Double-click their CQ line. WSJT-X fills in the DX Call, makes the messages and starts calling them.
  4. No CQ line yet? Paste the call into the DX Call box, press Generate Std Msgs, put your TX tone on a quiet spot (not on top of them) and turn on Enable Tx.
  5. When the contact finishes, log it in WSJT-X (or tap back to the Lounge tab and log it there).

A web page can't control WSJT-X itself (it only listens to programs on your own computer), so this page gives you everything to type in. If the Lounge's radio control (CAT) is on, turn it off first: WSJT-X needs the radio's port to itself.

📱 ON A PHONE

WSJT-X doesn't run on phones. Phone FT8 apps work the same way: tune to the dial frequency above and tap their CQ when it decodes. On Android, FT8CN is a popular free one; on an iPhone, search the App Store for FT8. Your phone needs an audio cable or interface to the radio.

A KPC FT8 program for phones in the field is on our to-do list.
