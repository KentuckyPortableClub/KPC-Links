ABOUT N4KPC
The club station

N4KPC is the club call of the Kentucky Portable Club. You will hear it from Kentucky parks, public lands and summits, at the KPC Campout and at club events.

ABOUT KPC
Kentucky Portable Club

A group of hams who take radio outdoors and explore the Commonwealth: lakes, rivers, forests, trails, caves, waterfalls, state parks and public lands.

New to portable or an old hand, a radio, an antenna, some power and a place to set up are all you need. Get outside. Get on the air. Explore Kentucky.
