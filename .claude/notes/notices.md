- **SITE-WIDE INFORMATION NOTICE (Brandon Oct 26 2:33 PM: "think that covers us on all occasions"; replaces the maps-only page; Oct 26 5:40 PM: no unreleased things named, so the app/logger/antenna-power wording became GUIDES, CALCULATORS AND TOOLS; Oct 26 8:55 PM (Brandon 'a'): OpenStreetMap outlines kept, labelled 'OpenStreetMap outline (unofficial)', never 'KPC-drawn'):** ONE PUBLIC page "About our information" (kpc-about-info.html, chat 1) holds the FULL text word for word, the CAPS words as section headings: "About our information. Everything on the Kentucky Portable Club website, tools and app is shared to help you plan and enjoy the hobby. We gather it carefully from official and trusted sources, but we can't guarantee it is complete, current or correct. Please use it as a starting point and check for yourself. MAPS AND PARKS. KPC's maps, park boundaries, access points, closures, campgrounds and park details are gathered from official agency sources and public data, and checked as carefully as we can. Even so, they are a starting point for your own planning, not a guarantee. We have official boundaries for most parks, but not all: some parks have no boundary map because the managing agency doesn't publish one, and some parks are only a single spot, such as a trailhead, dam or historic site. Where we have no official boundary, we say so. Some parks show an approximate OpenStreetMap outline, always marked unofficial. We keep adding more boundaries and official information all the time. Boundaries change, maps can be out of date or imprecise, and a pin or outline may not match what is on the ground. Private land, closures, seasonal rules and local regulations can apply even inside a park. Always check the managing agency's own official map and rules before you go, make sure you are on public land that is open to the public, and follow the rules of the program you are activating for. Each program sets its own rules, and activations run on the honor system: it's up to you to follow them. PARK DETAILS. Hours, fees, facilities, campgrounds, closures, hunting seasons and access notes come from the agencies' own pages and can change without notice. Check with the park or managing agency before you go. MEMBER AND VISITOR POSTS. Park notes, campground reviews, community posts and guestbook entries are written by members and visitors. They share personal experience, not official information, and may be out of date. LIVE INFORMATION. Spots, weather, alerts, radio conditions and propagation come from outside services and may be delayed, missing or wrong. Always use official weather warnings and your own judgment for safety. EVENTS AND CONTESTS. Dates, times and rules come from the organizers and can change. Check the organizer's own page before you plan around them. GUIDES, CALCULATORS AND TOOLS. Our guides, calculators and tools give general information and estimates, not professional advice. Follow your equipment's instructions, the FCC rules for your license, and safe practices around antennas, power and RF. Keep your own backups of your logs. OUTSIDE LINKS. We link to official and helpful sites, but we don't control them. You are responsible for your own location, safety, permission to be there and operating within your license. If you find something wrong, please let us know so we can fix it. KPC is an independent ham radio club, not part of Parks on the Air®, SOTA or WWFF." A link "About our information" goes in EVERY page footer (news.html → every page; chat 1, header/footer = chat 1 only). Always check the official agency map and rules before you go. [About our information]". Overlap tools carry the 2-FER notice below IN ADDITION.
