- No polygon named for the mystery unit: Washington DNR, South Florida WMD, Arkansas Game and Fish, New Jersey DEP, Missouri MDC, Michigan, Vermont (points only), Utah DWR.
- Nevada DOT has no scenic-byway layer.
- All 15 "no boundary exists" rows in the Nov 11 answers stand (listed in the JSON and marked solved in `solved-ledger.csv`).

The other way round (a miss proves nothing, look elsewhere):

- Tennessee's natural-areas layer is incomplete. PAD-US from USGS is the other official polygon.
