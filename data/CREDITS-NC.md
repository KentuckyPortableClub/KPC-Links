# Non-commercial source credits (NC)

Some official outlines on this site come from sources whose licence is **non-commercial (NC)**. These outlines are **not** covered by
this repository's general licence or terms. Each one keeps its own source's licence, and is labelled with it wherever it is shown or
given out.

| Source | Label shown with each outline | Licence | Layers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Western Australia, Department of Biodiversity, Conservation and Attractions (DBCA), on the public SLIP map service | **CC BY-NC 4.0, DBCA** | Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International | DBCA Legislated Lands and Waters (DBCA-011; the world key's 'WA DBCA state forests (legislated lands)'), Forest Blocks (DBCA-025), on `public-services.slip.wa.gov.au` |

Where the label is shown: the map popup's source line for every outline from one of these layers (kpc-bounds.js, `ncLabel`: any
layer on `public-services.slip.wa.gov.au`, or a layer whose label names DBCA), and this page.
