KPC FIELD TOOL

PRINTABLE PARK SHEET

Pick any POTA park in any state and print a one-page field sheet: QR codes to the official map, POTA page and directions, GPS, grid, how often it has been activated, the NOAA Weather Radio station for that county, places to stay, other programs at the same spot and the latest tips. Kentucky parks add parking points and 2-fers. Add a log sheet on the back.
