ABOUT KPC

Kentucky Portable Club is about getting outside, getting on the air, exploring new places, and building a community around portable amateur radio and adventure.

Who We Are

KPC — Kentucky Portable Club — was established in 2026 for people who enjoy taking amateur radio beyond the shack and into the outdoors.

We believe portable radio is better when it's part of an adventure. A park, campground, lake, trail, or weekend around the campfire can become the setting for making contacts, learning new skills, and spending time with good people.

KPC brings together portable operators, campers, explorers, and anyone interested in enjoying amateur radio outdoors. You don't have to have the biggest station or the most expensive equipment. The goal is to get outside, set up, operate, explore, and have a good time.
