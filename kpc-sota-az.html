GOOD TO KNOW

How the Zone Works

The 25-meter rule

Under SOTA rules your whole station — you, the radio and the antenna — has to be within 25 m (82 ft) of vertical height of the summit, in one connected area around the top. You need 4 contacts to qualify the summit.

How sure is this?

The zone comes from 90-meter elevation data, so it's a good guide, not a survey. Gentle, rounded Kentucky summits are easy; on a steep or narrow ridge, stay well up the hill. GPS altitude on a phone can be off by 10–20 m.

Kentucky summits

All Kentucky SOTA summits load instantly. For other states, tap "Load all U.S. summits" once on Wi-Fi. Check access before you go — some summits are on private land.
