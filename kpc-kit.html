POTA · WWFF · SOTA · ONE SCREEN

Activation Kit

Pick a park, WWFF area or summit (any state) and get everything for it on one screen: weather, the boundary map, the other references at the same spot, where to stay and a Spot Me button. Kentucky ones add member reports, parking and trailheads, and 2-fers. Save it before you go and it opens with no signal.
