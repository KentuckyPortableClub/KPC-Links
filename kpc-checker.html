Use our tools, then check for yourself. KPC builds these maps, guides and tools to help you plan, but we’re volunteers working from public data, and we make mistakes. Boundaries change, maps don’t always line up, and some land inside a park line is private. Before you activate, check the official agency map, confirm the land is public and open, and follow the rules of each program you’re activating. You’re responsible for making sure your activation is valid. Spot something wrong? Let us know so we can fix it.

PARKCOUNTYFIRSTACTS
Uses POTA's public activation records for the 187 Kentucky parks in the KPC guide. A park counts as activated with 10 or more QSOs in one activation. Nothing you type is saved or shared — the callsign is only used to filter POTA's public data in your browser.
Hunters: you need your hunter CSV file from POTA. POTA doesn't share hunter logs publicly, so the checker reads the file you download from POTA:
  1. Log in at pota.app and open My Stats.
  2. Download your hunter parks list as a CSV.
  3. Tap CHOOSE FILE here and pick that CSV.
You'll see every park you've hunted in the state you picked and every one you still need, on the map and in a list. Your file stays on your device.
