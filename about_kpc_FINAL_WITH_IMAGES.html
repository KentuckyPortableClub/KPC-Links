ABOUT KPC

Kentucky Portable Club is about getting outside, getting on the air, exploring new places, and building a community around portable amateur radio and adventure.

Who We Are

KPC — Kentucky Portable Club — was established in 2026 for people who enjoy taking amateur radio beyond the shack and into the outdoors.

OPERATE

Portable Ham Radio

Take amateur radio into the field and build practical portable operating skills.

CAMP

Camping

Combine camping and radio with weekend trips, campouts, and outdoor gatherings.

EXPLORE

Adventure

Discover Kentucky parks, public lands, trails, lakes, and new operating locations.

BELONG

Community

Share knowledge, help new operators, make contacts, and enjoy good fellowship.

Built Around The Adventure

We believe portable radio is better when it is part of an adventure. A park, campground, lake, trail, or weekend around the campfire can become the setting for making contacts, learning new skills, and spending time with good people.

You don't need the biggest station or the most expensive equipment. The goal is simple: get outside, set up, operate, explore, and have a good time.
