HOW IT SOUNDS ON THE AIR

Typical CW Exchanges

There's no required POTA exchange — but almost everyone uses this short format. Tap ▶ to hear any line (pick the speed above). Ready to try it yourself? Head to the CW Practice Room.

POTA — You're the Hunter

W4XYZ is in a park. You're K4TEST in Kentucky.

  1. ACTIVATORCQ POTA CQ POTA DE W4XYZ W4XYZ K
  2. YOUK4TEST
  3. ACTIVATORK4TEST GM UR 5NN 5NN TN TN BK
  4. YOUBK TU UR 5NN 5NN KY KY BK
  5. ACTIVATORTU KY 73 E E
  6. YOUE E

Send your call once, and don't add a K after it (W1AW K sounds like W1AWK). The park number usually isn't sent — it's on the spot. "5NN" is 599 with cut numbers.

POTA — You're the Activator

You're K4TEST in a Kentucky park. Hunters call you.

  1. YOUCQ POTA CQ POTA DE K4TEST K4TEST K
  2. HUNTERSN8ABC   W4QZ   KD9XYZ
  3. YOUW4QZ GM UR 5NN 5NN KY KY BK
  4. HUNTERBK R TU UR 5NN 5NN VA VA BK
  5. YOUTU VA 73 E E
  6. HUNTERE E

Only got part of a call? Send it with a question mark — KD9? — and only that station should answer. QRZ? asks everyone. You need 10 contacts for a POTA activation.

SOTA — Summit Activator & Chaser

SOTA reports are often real numbers, and the summit is sent in the exchange.

  1. ACTIVATORCQ SOTA CQ SOTA DE N4XX/P K
  2. CHASERK4TEST
  3. ACTIVATORK4TEST UR 559 559 W4K/EC-001 BK
  4. CHASERBK R TU UR 579 579 KY BK
  5. ACTIVATORTU 73 E E

A SOTA activation needs 4 contacts. Summit-to-summit stations go first — if you hear "S2S", step aside.

Park-to-Park & Summit-to-Summit

Both stations are out — trade references so both logs count.

  1. ACTIVATORCQ POTA DE W4XYZ K
  2. YOUP2P K4TEST
  3. ACTIVATORK4TEST UR 5NN TN US-2981 US-2981 BK
  4. YOUBK TU 5NN KY US-1271 US-1271 BK
  5. ACTIVATORTU 73 E E

Need their reference? Send PARK? (or REF? / SUMMIT?). Put your own park or summit in the box at the top of the Practice Room to practice P2P/S2S.

CW Clubs, Classes & Apps

The groups and tools KPC hams use to learn Morse, get faster, and find people to work. Most classes are free and online.

CLASSES • ALL LEVELS

Long Island CW Club

Small live online classes from your very first letter up to advanced, taught by volunteers. A big, friendly club with practice sessions most days of the week — despite the name, members are everywhere.

LONGISLANDCWCLUB.ORG ↗ CLASSES • BEGINNER TO FAST

CWops & CW Academy

CW Academy runs free semester-style classes, Beginner through Advanced. CWops is the speed crowd — their weekly CWT mini-contests are the place to push your WPM.

CWOPS.ORG ↗ STRAIGHT KEYS & BUGS

Straight Key Century Club

SKCC — mechanical keys only (straight keys, bugs, cooties), relaxed speeds, a free member number, monthly sprints and plenty of awards to chase.

SKCCGROUP.COM ↗ 2-METER CW • JUST FOR FUN

Brass Knuckle Gang

BKG — CW on 2 meters only. Work an existing member on 144 MHz CW and you get "jumped in" with your own BKG number. Watch for members on POTA and SOTA activations — a Yagi on a summit goes a long way.

BKG.CLUB ↗ QRP • LOW POWER

North American QRP CW Club

NAQCC — CW at 5 watts or less, a natural fit for portable POTA and SOTA. Free to join, with monthly sprints and challenges.

NAQCC.INFO ↗ PILEUP PRACTICE

Morse Walker

Browser pileup trainer with POTA, CWT and SST formats. Great after our page — it throws whole pileups at you to pick calls out of.

MORSEWALKER.COM ↗ FREE ONLINE COURSE

LCWO — Learn CW Online

Free Koch-method lessons in your browser, plus callsign and word training. Tracks your progress lesson by lesson.

LCWO.NET ↗ FREE AUDIO

Morse Code Ninja

Thousands of free practice files and YouTube videos at every speed — QSOs, POTA exchanges, words and callsigns. Perfect for the truck.

MORSECODE.NINJA ↗
PHONE APP • GAME

Morse Mania

Learn Morse as a game on your phone — hundreds of short levels for letters, numbers, words and sending. A fun way to fill five minutes.

LIVE CW ONLINE

Vail & VBand

Key CW to real people over the internet — no radio needed. Join the KPC room on Vail (steps in the Practice Room) or VBand's CW parties.
