GRID & COUNTY

Get the grid square for any spot on Earth, the county (Kentucky and the rest of the US), the province in Canada or the country elsewhere — for where you're standing, a tap on the map, any park number or any town — plus the nearest parks.

Kentucky counties come from Kentucky county boundaries kept in the page (works offline); other US counties come from the FCC's census-area lookup, Canadian provinces and other countries from OpenStreetMap (both need a connection). Near a county line, check the official map. Grid squares are 6-character Maidenhead locators. Parks are measured to the park's POTA point, so a big park can be closer than it looks. Tap the map to look up another spot.
