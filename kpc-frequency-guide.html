Frequency Quick Reference

US amateur radio bands, commonly recognized activity frequencies, and operating-mode references — built for quick use at home or in the field.

Important operating reminder

Activity frequencies are customary, not exclusive assignments. Listen first and check whether a frequency is in use. Your license class, location, mode, bandwidth and power limits determine where you may transmit. Consult the current FCC rules and ARRL band plan before operating.
