KENTUCKY TRAILS

Five Trails Worth Packing The Radio For

Official trail maps, access and alerts for Kentucky's major trails. Where a trail passes through another POTA park, it is listed as a possible 2-fer — stand on the trail inside the other park, and confirm both boundaries on the official maps before you count it.

01 • US-11181 • NATIONAL RECREATION TRAIL

Sheltowee Trace

Kentucky's long-distance trail through the Daniel Boone National Forest, from Rowan County south into Big South Fork. Use the Sheltowee Trace Association section maps and the Forest Service trail guide.

KENTUCKY OFFICE OF NATURE PRESERVES

Kentucky's Nine Wild River Corridors

Only designated sections are Kentucky Wild Rivers — not the entire length of each named river. Use the official Wild Rivers map to view the state-designated corridors, and the access links for locations confirmed by land managers.

Important boundary and access notice: Kentucky Wild River corridors protect specific reaches and adjacent visible lands, with corridor limits set by state law. Many banks and access routes are privately owned. A Wild River designation does not automatically establish a separate POTA reference, a public access right, or a valid 2-fer.

Within Mammoth Cave National Park.

Before You Go

Verify current conditions. Check access, closures, permits, campground status, hunting restrictions, seasonal conditions, activation boundaries, weather, and local rules with the current land manager before travel or portable operation. Possible 2-fer — verify before activating: overlapping maps or nearby trail/river routes do not establish eligibility for two programs. WMAs and public hunting areas: check season dates before you go and wear blaze orange during gun seasons.
