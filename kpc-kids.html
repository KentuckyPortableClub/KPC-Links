🔤 Phonetic Match

Hams say Alpha for A and Bravo for B so letters don't get mixed up on the radio. Can you match them?

🎙️ Spell Your Name

Type your first name (or a call sign) and hear how a ham would spell it on the radio. Then say it out loud yourself!

🕵️ Call Sign Detective

A radio station says its call sign in phonetics. Listen carefully and type the call sign. Every ham has one!

📻 Morse Code Fun

Before microphones, radio operators talked with beeps: short ones (dots) and long ones (dashes). Hams still do it today!
