Radio control

Not connected.

Profiles keep one radio's whole setup (type, speed, port, how it is keyed) under a name: home rig, field rig. Hamlib's rigctld and flrig are programs on the PC that know 300+ radios; pick one of them here when the radio is not in MY RADIO (rigctld needs the Windows app).

On a phone or tablet only these two ways of keying work: the CAT command, or the radio's own VOX. The RTS and DTR lines have to hold the USB port, and holding it stops the tablet sending sound to the radio. Set the radio to DATA mode with DATA MOD SOURCE = USB.

🔧 RADIO CHECK SCREEN (step-by-step test)

Sound

off

TX LEVEL: press TUNE on the FT8 screen and come down until the radio's ALC barely moves and the watts are full. Still not enough at 0 dB? Turn this device's media volume all the way up, then the radio's USB MOD GAIN. RX LEVEL: turn it up if the waterfall looks dark and the meter barely moves, down if the meter turns red (too loud). The radio's own USB OUT level does the same job, so start at 0 dB. Both are also in ⚙ FT8 on the FT8 screen.

Log files

No uploads turned on.

ADIF is the file POTA, WWFF, SOTA, LoTW and QRZ all take (a 2-fer on your side = one file per park). UPLOADS sends each contact to QRZ, eQSL or Wavelog by itself when there is signal. The KPC log file carries every contact for every operator: save it here, ADD it on your other phone or PC, and the two logs merge with no doubles. The log also stays in the app until you clear it.

Parks on this device

Download a state once while you have signal; the parks stay on this device. Kentucky comes with every park's outline (official, or marked approximate); other states come with pins for now. AM I IN THE PARK works from GPS with no signal.

Alerts & help

Clock: checking…

FT8 needs this device's clock right within about a second. If it drifts, turn on "set time automatically" in Android or Windows settings.

How the FT8 screen works: it listens to the radio's sound and shows everyone it hears every 15 seconds (FT4: 7.5 s). Tap a line to call that station — the six messages fill in and TX turns on. With Auto on it answers by itself (grid → report → R-report → RR73 → 73) and the contact drops into your log. Decoder: ft8_lib by Karlis Goba (MIT licence).

Something wrong? Press that, paste the text to whoever is fixing the app: version, device, settings, radio and the last messages. Nothing secret goes in it.
