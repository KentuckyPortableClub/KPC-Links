Natural Earth
=============

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.

Natural Earth's map data is public domain (see the Terms of Use page on
naturalearthdata.com). Anyone may use, change and share it without asking.
A credit is not required; the line above is given as a thank-you.

Used in the KPC app: www/kpc-world.js, the built-in world map for use with no
signal (1:110m land and country borders; 1:50m state and province lines for the
US, Canada, Mexico and Australia; coordinates rounded to 0.05 degrees).
