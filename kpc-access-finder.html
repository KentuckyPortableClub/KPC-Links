PARKING & ACCESS FINDER

Type any POTA park in any state, or pick a Kentucky property, and see mapped parking, entrances, gates, trailheads and possible roadside pull-offs. Points come from official Kentucky GIS (in Kentucky) and OpenStreetMap, not guessed from the boundary.

🅿️ Parking lots🚪 Entrances & gates🥾 Trailheads🚗 Pull-offs
Use our tools, then check for yourself. KPC builds these maps, guides and tools to help you plan, but we’re volunteers working from public data, and we make mistakes. Boundaries change, maps don’t always line up, and some land inside a park line is private. Before you activate, check the official agency map, confirm the land is public and open, and follow the rules of each program you’re activating. You’re responsible for making sure your activation is valid. Spot something wrong? Let us know so we can fix it.

Outside Kentucky the park outline comes from the national public-lands map (USGS PAD-US) and the parking from OpenStreetMap, a volunteer map: popular parks are usually well mapped, remote ones may be thin. Or pick a Kentucky property below for the extra state sources.

Mapped points are not proof of legal parking or public entry. An entrance may be a general navigation point, not a parking area. A possible pull-off must be inspected on satellite imagery and verified on site. Observe agency restrictions and do not block gates or roads.

Sources: KPC boundary GeoJSON, Kentucky Fish & Wildlife GIS (entrances and WMA map points), OpenStreetMap mapped parking/trailheads/lay-bys. The live sources may be incomplete or unavailable. Satellite imagery © Esri and providers; terrain © OpenTopoMap contributors.
