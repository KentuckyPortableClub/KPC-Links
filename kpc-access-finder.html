Mapped points are not proof of legal parking or public entry. An entrance may be a general navigation point, not a parking area. A possible pull-off must be inspected on satellite imagery and verified on site. Observe agency restrictions and do not block gates or roads.

Sources: KPC boundary GeoJSON, Kentucky Fish & Wildlife GIS (entrances and WMA map points), OpenStreetMap mapped parking/trailheads/lay-bys. The live sources may be incomplete or unavailable. Satellite imagery © Esri and providers; terrain © OpenTopoMap contributors.
