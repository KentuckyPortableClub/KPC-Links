WHAT IT MEANS

RF Exposure in Plain English

Two kinds of people

Controlled means you and people who know they're near a transmitter and can control it — your household, your club members at the table. Uncontrolled is everyone else: neighbors, park visitors walking by. The limit for the public is about five times stricter.

Why average power?

The limits are averaged over time — 6 minutes for controlled, 30 minutes for the public. SSB voice is only "on" about 20% of the time, and you listen about half the time, so your average is far below your peak power. FT8 and FM are full power whenever you transmit.

Portable tips

  • Small magnetic loops have a very strong field close in. Keep extra distance and never touch one while transmitting. See mag loops.
  • Put the antenna up high or out away from the table and trail
  • QRP (5 W) is almost always fine anywhere
  • Don't let anyone touch the antenna while transmitting — RF burns are real
  • Running 100 W on a whip next to your chair? Check it here
