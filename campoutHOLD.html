MEET & GREET

Meet fellow campers, make new friends and put faces to the callsigns and people you’ve connected with along the way.

FOX HUNT

Put your radio-direction-finding skills to the test as you track down the hidden signal and enjoy some friendly competition.

POTA ACTIVATIONS

Get on the air from Taylorsville Lake, make contacts, hunt activations and share the excitement of Parks on the Air.

FELLOWSHIP

Relax around camp, swap stories, compare gear, talk radios and enjoy time with friends from Kentucky and beyond.

COMMUNITY MEAL

A special opportunity for everyone to gather around the table for good food, conversation and fellowship.

GROUP PHOTOS

We’ll gather together for group photos and memories of the people who make KPC what it is.

CHALLENGES & GAMES

Expect fun challenges, games and a little friendly competition. You may leave with some serious bragging rights!

2027 DATES SET

2027 KYPOTA Campout

Mark your calendar for September 24–26, 2027. The 2027 KYPOTA Campout is headed to Taylorsville Lake State Park for a weekend built around getting outside, getting on the air, and spending time with the KPC community.

Get Outside

Camp, explore, relax, and enjoy a Kentucky state park setting with fellow KPC members.

Get On The Air

Bring your portable station and enjoy amateur radio from the outdoors. Activation details will be announced later.

Fellowship

Meet other operators, swap stories, share knowledge, and enjoy the weekend together.
