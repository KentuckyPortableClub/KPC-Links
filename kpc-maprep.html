MAPPING DESK

Search the official layers

United States first. Canada is on: pick a province, or CA for the national CPCAD layer. Other countries are in the catalog and are not queried. No drawing tools. A lead export is a candidate list, not a boundary.

Pin vs this uses the pin on the map and does not need a name. Parks you could add lists public land near that pin that is not already a POTA park. Export leads downloads that list. It does not download outlines.

Tract notes show the pieces, the parent, and parks inside parks when that state’s tract file is on the site. Kentucky’s tract file is not on the site yet.
