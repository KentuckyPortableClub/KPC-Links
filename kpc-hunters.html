🏅 What the badges on a spot mean

They show how close this contact gets you to a POTA hunter award. They come from your own POTA numbers, so tap ⭐ GET MY PARKS first (and again now and then to keep them fresh).

🔁 Park 14/20

Repeat Offender. You've worked this park 14 times; the award comes every 20 contacts with the same park. Working it now makes 15.

🤝 K4TEST 37/50

Operator to Operator. You've worked this activator 37 times (at any park); the award comes every 50 contacts with the same person.

📶 NEW BAND 4/10

N1CC. You've worked this park on 4 bands and this spot is on a band you don't have there yet. The award is one park on 10 bands.

🔁 Park 17/20 · ALMOST

Glowing gold = almost there. Within 5 of the next Repeat Offender level, within 10 of the next Op to Op level, or any new N1CC band.

On the table view the numbers show without the words. POTA decides the awards; these are just a heads-up.

📄 TAP TO PICK YOUR FILEor drop it here · ADIF (or CSV) · POTA, WWFF and SOTA

Where do I get the file?

  • Easiest for POTA: your hunted parks from POTA. Sign in at pota.app → My Stats → your hunted parks list → ⬇ EXPORT CSV. You get hunter_parks.csv: pick it above. Do it again every couple of weeks; the page reminds you.
  • Or your logbook as an ADIF file (all three programs): from your QRZ Logbook (Logbook → Settings → Export → ADIF) or your logging program. The page reads the POTA, WWFF / KFF and SOTA numbers saved on each contact, so it only knows the parks you logged with their number.
  • SOTA: your chaser log from sotadata.org.uk (CSV) works too.
  • Logged here? Contacts you log in this Lounge count right away, no file needed.
  • Old POTA numbers like K-1234 count as US-1234.
Will it remember me? Yes, on this phone or computer. Your list is saved in this web browser, not on GitHub or anywhere else, so it's still here when you come back in 4 days (or 4 months). It's gone if you clear your browser's data or use a private window, and a different phone needs its own upload. Your log only changes when you upload again, so do that every week or two. Logging a contact here adds that park right away.

Far-away new parks are harder to work, so they can go to the small ticker at the very top instead of the big one. Set where you are and how far you usually hear.

📻 RADIO CONTROL Plug your radio in by USB: tap a spot and the radio jumps to its frequency and mode. It never transmits.
This browser can't talk to a radio. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) with the radio plugged in by USB. Newer Chrome on Android phones can too (USB-C cable). iPhones and iPads can't do this from a web page yet.
WHICH PORT IS MY RADIO?

Picked the wrong one? Tap the right port here, or pick a different one. Not sure which? Unplug the radio's USB cable, see which port goes away, plug it back in.

SETTINGS FOR MY RADIO (filled in when you pick one)

The speed must match your radio's menu (Icom: CI-V Baud Rate · Yaesu: CAT RATE · Kenwood: COM speed). Icom: the CI-V address must match the radio's CI-V Address menu.

Not connected yet.
  • Plug the radio into the computer with its USB cable. Some radios need a driver first (Icom IC-7300 / 705 and many Yaesus: the Silicon Labs CP210x driver from the radio maker's site).
  • Close other radio programs (WSJT-X, flrig, Ham Radio Deluxe, the radio's own app). Only one program can use the radio's port at a time.
  • Press CONNECT MY RADIO and pick the radio's port in the box Chrome shows (Yaesu radios show two: pick the Enhanced one). After that, CONNECT finds the port your radio answers on by itself, and you can name each port under CHANGE PORT.
  • Press TEST: READ MY DIAL. If it shows your frequency, you're set: tap any spot below and the radio goes there.

⚠ Clicking a spot only changes your radio's frequency and mode. It never transmits. Check the frequency is inside your license privileges before you call.
