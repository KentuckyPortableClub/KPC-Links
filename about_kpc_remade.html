About KPC

KPC is a group of outdoor-minded people who enjoy amateur radio, camping, exploring Kentucky's parks and public lands, and building a community along the way. Whether you're a seasoned operator or just getting started, KPC is a place to make friends, share knowledge, get outside, and enjoy what Kentucky has to offer.

👥

Our Community

A welcoming group of operators, campers, and outdoor enthusiasts from Kentucky and beyond.

📡

Our Passion

Amateur radio, portable operations, and making contacts from Kentucky's beautiful outdoors.

⛰️

Our Outdoors

Exploring state parks, wildlife areas, and public lands across the Commonwealth.

🤝

Our Mission

Bring people together through outdoor adventure, amateur radio, and community.

Kentucky Roots

From the Red River Gorge to Mammoth Cave, from Cumberland Falls to Kentucky Lake, our state is filled with places worth exploring. KPC celebrates those places through portable radio, camping, and fellowship.
