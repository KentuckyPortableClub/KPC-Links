Side view (elevation)

Distance from the center = signal strength. Low angles near the ground line reach far away (DX). High angles straight up fill in nearby (NVIS).

Estimated SWR across the band

Dotted line is 2:1. Tuned for your frequency when the wire length is the calculated length. Shorten the wire to move the dip higher in frequency; lengthen it to move lower.

How to read this (and how far to trust it) This is a quick estimator using standard antenna theory (ground reflection with real-ground numbers, simple feed-point models), not a full NEC simulation. Expect gain within about 1–2 dB and SWR that is right in shape but not in exact numbers. Trees, nearby metal, coax length, a feedline choke and your actual ground change real results, so always check with an antenna analyzer. Later versions will add linked dipoles and side-by-side comparison.
