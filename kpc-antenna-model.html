How to read this (and how far to trust it) This is a quick estimator using standard antenna theory (ground reflection with real-ground numbers, simple feed-point models), not a full NEC simulation. Expect gain within about 1–2 dB and SWR that is right in shape but not in exact numbers. Trees, nearby metal, coax length, a feedline choke and your actual ground change real results, so always check with an antenna analyzer. Random-wire and multiband end-fed numbers use a simple wire-as-transmission-line model: they show which bands are easy or hard, not exact SWR. Coax loss uses the same published cable figures as the Antenna Calculator. Working on a vertical? See how many radials and how long.
