KPC PARK TRACKER

PARK TRACKER

Every POTA park you have activated, in any state, Canadian province or country, on a map, and the ones still to go. Load the file from POTA's My Stats page; in Kentucky your call sign is enough.

Activators LoungePark TrackerRare ParksActivation KitAm I in the Park?
1. Load your POTA file
  1. Go to pota.app and sign in.
  2. Open My Stats, find your activated parks list and tap ⬇ EXPORT CSV. You get a file called activator_parks.csv (it lands in your Downloads).
  3. Tap PICK MY POTA FILE below and choose that file. An ADIF activator log from your logging program works too.
  4. Do it again every month or so, so new activations show up. The page reminds you.

Your file stays on this phone or computer: nothing is uploaded. Park lists for every state, Canada and 64 other countries come from POTA and are refreshed every night. In Kentucky, a park counts as activated with 10 or more contacts in one activation (POTA's rule).
