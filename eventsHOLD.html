JUNE 5–6, 2027 • KPC EVENT

Happy Birthday, Kentucky!

Kentucky became the 15th state on June 1, 1792. Every year on the first weekend after its birthday, KPC heads out to activate Kentucky parks and celebrate — 235 years in 2027. Saturday is also the Kentucky QSO Party, so hunters will be listening for Kentucky. Pick a park, get on the air, and wish the Commonwealth a happy birthday.

PARKS ON THE AIR

Support Your Parks Weekends — 2027

Four weekends a year, POTA activators head out to support their parks. Pick a Kentucky park, get on the air, and help a hunter fill a log. Times run in UTC — check pota.app for the official rules and any changes.
