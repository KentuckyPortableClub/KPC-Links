KENTUCKY PORTABLE CLUB

WHO WE ARE

We combine amateur radio, outdoor adventure, and Kentucky's incredible public lands to build a welcoming portable-radio community.

THE KPC MISSION

Radio. Adventure. Community.

Everything on this page uses the same typography, spacing, colors, navigation, and responsive behavior established on the redesigned homepage.

01

Outdoor Adventure

We combine amateur radio with camping, hiking, and exploring Kentucky's parks, lakes, forests, trails, and public lands.

02

Portable Operations

We promote POTA, SOTA, field day operations, and all forms of portable amateur radio. Take your radio outside and discover what's possible.

03

Kentucky's Beauty

We showcase Kentucky's state parks, wildlife management areas, lakes, rivers, caves, waterfalls, and scenic locations through amateur radio activations.

04

Community

We bring people together — locally and from across the region — to share knowledge, make new friends, and enjoy the outdoors while operating from amazing Kentucky locations.

OUR PURPOSE

Promoting Kentucky

KPC is committed to highlighting and promoting Kentucky's state parks, forests, lakes, trails, caves, waterfalls, and natural beauty. Through portable radio operations, exploration, and online content, we help showcase the incredible locations our state has to offer.

OUR FUTURE

Growing Portable Operations

We want to get more people involved in portable amateur radio by making it approachable, fun, and rewarding. Whether you're new to the hobby or a seasoned operator, KPC encourages everyone to take their radio outside and experience the thrill of operating from Kentucky's great outdoors.
