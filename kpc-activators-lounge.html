📄 My POTA file: load it once, every tool knows

Which parks have you already activated? Load your file from POTA here and the Park Tracker, Rarely Activated (hide mine), the Top 25 and the trip planners (NEW TO ME) all use it, for every state and country. It stays on this phone or computer.

  1. Sign in at pota.app and open My Stats.
  2. In your activated parks list tap ⬇ EXPORT CSV (you get activator_parks.csv).
  3. Tap the button below and pick that file. Do it again every month or so.
