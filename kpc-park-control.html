KENTUCKY PARK CONTROL

Every Kentucky POTA park, KFF (WWFF) reference and W4K SOTA summit on one map. Search by name, number or town, tap a dot for directions, official links, parking and park tips, and put a trip together.

🌲 195 POTA parks🌳 KFF references⛰ 216 SOTA summits🧭 Trip list

👆 Zoomed in, tap an outline to see what land it is (park name, POTA / KFF / SOTA numbers, or government land with no park number); tap more › for the details. 📌 To drop a pin, press and hold the map (right-click on a computer) or use DROP A PIN: then STREET VIEW, DIRECTIONS, COPY GPS or + ADD THIS SPOT TO MY TRIP.

PICK A PARK

Tap a dot on the map or search above. Green = POTA park, teal = KFF reference, orange = SOTA summit.

🧭 MY TRIP

Tap + ADD TO MY TRIP on a park, or drop a pin, to start a trip.

Pins and outlines are guides only. Confirm every boundary on the official map before you set up. Kentucky only: KPC's checked Kentucky data (official websites, maps, parking points from the land managers). Park tips and places to stay come from KPC members.
