ABOUT KPC

The Kentucky Portable Club (KPC) is a community built around portable ham radio, camping, parks, adventure, and fellowship.

Whether you're an experienced operator or just getting started, KPC is about getting outside, getting on the air, and connecting with others across Kentucky and beyond.
