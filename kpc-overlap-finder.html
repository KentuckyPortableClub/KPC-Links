Please read: this is a research aid, not a ruling. The Park Overlap Finder shows places where our maps suggest the boundaries of two or more POTA parks may overlap. It is a starting point for your own research only. The Kentucky Portable Club cannot confirm or deny that any location listed here is a valid multi-park activation site. Park boundaries change, maps can be out of date or imprecise, and private land, closures and local rules may apply even inside a boundary. Before you count an activation, check each park's official boundary map from the managing agency, make sure you are on public land that is open to the public, and follow POTA's activation rules. POTA sets the rules, and activations run on the honor system: it's up to each activator to follow them. You are responsible for confirming your own location. KPC is an independent ham radio club, not part of Parks on the Air®.

THE PARKS + THE OVERLAP (tap to switch each on or off)

    📍 GPS walk-inTap 📍 GPS WALK-IN at the park: the card tells you what our maps show where you stand. Or 📌 DROP A PIN and tap the map.
    ▸ HOW WE CHECK (in plain words)
    • Official maps only: each park's own agency boundary first; national databases next; volunteer maps never confirm a spot.
    • Land spots: the overlap must fit a 50-yard circle inside every park. 100+ yards across = listed; 50–100 yards = listed with "small overlap: tight fit"; 25–50 yards = a separate "very tight: could be map error" list; under 25 yards = not listed.
    • Only land the park really owns or manages counts, never private land inside a park line and never an outer "purchase" line. Two owners side by side is not a 2-fer.
    • A trail or river that is only a line uses POTA's 100-ft rule: the light band along the line IS the zone.
    • Water-only spots are listed apart (boat or kayak).
    • We try hard, and we can still be wrong.
