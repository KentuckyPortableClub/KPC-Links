KPC website: notices for the parts made by other people
=======================================================

The Kentucky Portable Club website is made by the club. A few parts inside it
were made by other people and are used under their own licences. Those
licences ask that this notice travels with them, so it lives here.


1. ft8_lib  (the FT8 / FT4 decoder and encoder on the FT8 screens)
   ----------------------------------------------------------------
   By Karlis Goba. MIT licence.
   Full text: ft8_lib-MIT.txt in this folder.
   Source:    https://github.com/kgoba/ft8_lib
   On the site: kpc-ft8.wasm and kpc-ft8-wasm.js (the same build as the KPC
   app, made by .claude/build/ft8/build.sh with the small changes listed in
   that script).


Other things the site uses
--------------------------
- Map pictures and map tiles are loaded over the internet from their owners
  and are credited on the map whenever they show. They are not stored here.
